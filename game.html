Exclusive Features

Main Features

Explore the pillars that make Poke Idle World an unforgettable experience — where adventure, strategy and nostalgia meet in a single universe.

EVOLUTION
Evolution System
Awaken your Pokémon’s true power. Evolve by level or with stones (Fire, Water, Thunder, Leaf…) and reach higher forms.
  • Branching evolution lines, like Eevee
  • Evolution by level or by stone
  • Stats that grow with unique IVs and nature
CAPTURE
Capture & Pokédex
Weaken the wild Pokémon and catch it with the right poké ball. Every capture is unique, with its own IVs, nature and gender.
  • Chance based on HP, level and ball
  • IVs (1–31) and nature set at capture
  • National Pokédex with Kanto’s 151
IDLE
Idle Combat & Progress
Your leader Pokémon hunts on its own with its real moves — even while you’re offline. Come back and collect XP, gold and new captures.
  • The leader uses the best move per type and level
  • Effectiveness, STAB and damage true to the game
  • The hunt goes on even when you’re away
COMMUNITY
Community and Trade
Move the economy with the player market, trade items, stones and poké balls, and compete for the top-trainer ranking.
  • Free market (gold or diamonds)
  • A living in-game economy
  • Competitive trainer ranking
